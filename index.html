Why SafeReturn Was Created

Last year, I got separated from my family in a crowded temple. For a few terrifying minutes, I had absolutely no idea where they were. The crowd kept moving, people were everywhere, and despite trying to search for them, I felt completely lost and helpless. Even though it may not sound like a long time, in that moment it felt endless. What scared me most was realizing how quickly someone can disappear in a crowded place — especially children, elderly people, or vulnerable individuals. After finally reuniting with my family, I couldn’t stop thinking about that experience. I kept wondering how many families go through situations far worse than mine every single day, and how difficult it must be when someone is not found quickly. Since that day, I secretly started working on an idea that could genuinely help people during emergencies. That idea slowly became SafeReturn. SafeReturn was built as an AI-powered emergency response platform designed to help reunite missing people and families faster using realtime alerts, AI face recognition, GPS systems, emergency SOS broadcasts, CCTV evidence uploads, volunteer coordination, and rescue tools. This project is not just a school project to me. It is something deeply personal that I have been quietly building ever since that experience, with the hope that technology can help prevent families from experiencing the same fear and panic that I felt that day.
